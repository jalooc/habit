Decision · two sheets

Sheet A — Form (Add + Edit).  Sheet B — Reader (Presentation).

You were right: detent-switching between read and write was a UX trick papering over the fact that they're different components. Read has no fields and no Save/Cancel — it's just rendered content. Add and Edit, on the other hand, are structurally identical: same three fields, same button pair. They become one form with two modes.
